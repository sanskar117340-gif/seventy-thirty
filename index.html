        Legal

        Privacy policy

        Last updated November 13, 2026.

        What Seventy Thirty stores

        The name, age, gender and currency you enter when you create a profile, and every amount, note and date you log afterwards.

        Where it is stored

        Everything is saved in your browser's local storage on this device. Seventy Thirty does not send your profile or entries to a server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

        Fonts

        The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

        Shared devices

        There is no password. Anyone who uses this browser profile can open your ledger by entering the same name. Do not use it on a device you share unless you sign out and delete your data afterwards.

        Your choices

        • Remove any single entry from the History tab.
        • Sign out to leave the ledger. Your data stays on this device.
        • Delete everything below, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

        Using Seventy Thirty

        Seventy Thirty is a personal tool for tracking savings with a 70% spend and 30% save guideline. You may use it for your own record keeping.

        The split, ranks and badges are budgeting aids. They are not financial, tax or legal advice, and every money decision remains yours.

        Your records

        You enter all figures yourself. The app does not connect to banks or check what you type. Records live in your browser, so clearing site data or switching devices can remove them. Keep your own backup of anything important.

        Ranks and badges

        Ranks and badges are game features for motivation. They have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for anything.
